How to Play:

  • Press "Start Game" to begin
  • Use Arrow Keys or WASD to control the snake direction
  • Press 1-9 keys or buttons to change speed (9 is fastest)
  • Press Shift or the Autopilot button to toggle Autopilot mode
  • Press Alt or the Auto Clicker button to toggle Auto Clicker mode
  • The world is endless!
  • Collect food to grow and score points
  • You are invincible!
